# Hero footage

`hero-1600.mp4`, `hero-960.mp4` and `poster.webp` are a trimmed, re-encoded
9-second loop made from a free stock clip (top-down aerial of a forest river):

- Source: https://www.pexels.com/video/an-aerial-view-of-a-river-flowing-through-a-forest-24837087/
- Licence: Pexels Licence, https://www.pexels.com/license/ (free to use and
  modify; attribution not required, given here anyway)

The clip shows a river, **not the Yamuna**. It is illustrative artwork, not
monitoring imagery or cleanup evidence, and the interface labels it as such.

Media in this folder is not covered by the repository's MIT licence; it
remains under the Pexels Licence. To swap in your own footage, replace the
three files and keep the names.
