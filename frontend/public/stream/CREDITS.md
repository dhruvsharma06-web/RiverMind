# Stream footage

`stream-1600.mp4`, `stream-960.mp4` and `poster.webp` are a trimmed,
re-encoded 9-second seamless loop (crossfaded ends) made from a free stock
clip of clear water running over stones:

- Source: https://www.pexels.com/video/crystal-clear-water-flowing-over-rocks-33733737/
  (Jack And Matt Photography)
- Licence: Pexels Licence, https://www.pexels.com/license/ (free to use and
  modify; attribution not required, given here anyway)

The clip shows a stream, **not the Yamuna**. It is illustrative artwork, not
monitoring imagery or cleanup evidence, and the interface labels it as such.

Media in this folder is not covered by the repository's MIT licence; it
remains under the Pexels Licence.
